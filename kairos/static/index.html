Fees, execution & paper margin

Default: full starting allocation, fee reserve, and reinvestment; no scheduled end or profit target. With reinvestment enabled, order and exposure caps scale in proportion to equity versus starting capital. Save a new paper balance, then reset the paper portfolio to apply it.

Capital recovery is spot-only: above 2× original equity, sell enough inventory if needed and reserve the original allocation once. Reserved cash remains on Kraken but is excluded from this bot. No external withdrawal is made.

100 bps = 1%. Paper fills are estimates, not a backtest. Margin uses a simplified cross-collateral model, not Kraken’s exact borrowing or liquidation rules.

US stocks / ETFs status

Direct US stocks are not xStocks. Kraken offers equities in its app, but the linked public Exchange API does not document US brokerage order submission. Stock execution is blocked until Kraken provides that API. No tokenized substitute is used.
