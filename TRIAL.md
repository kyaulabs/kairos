# Multi-bar paper trial

[`kairos/trial.json`](kairos/trial.json) registers `alpaca-btc-multibar-001` before prospective execution. Its hash is recorded with each trial run. The strategy uses completed Alpaca US hourly bars, deterministic entries and the existing independent exits. Jev is not called and cannot authorize or veto an order. This is a new experiment, not a repair that establishes profitability.

The trial lasts 14 calendar days from the user's first Start. Stop, outages and service restarts do not extend it. A completed bar can establish a structural candidate even when the current execution quote fails the cost gate. That candidate retains its original target, price bounds and three-hour expiry. Quotes cannot create a candidate, extend its life or revive a consumed attempt. Start/recovery retires pending candidates and establishes a new completed-bar baseline. A rising-trend failure invalidates a pending candidate. Each submission attempt consumes its candidate, including an unfilled or rejected attempt.

Keep the $500 allocation, $25 order ceiling, $100 exposure ceiling and $12.50 daily-loss halt. The 80% exposure target, 100% trim trigger, 300-bps stop, seven-day holding limit and 10-second source-age gate remain. Position deadlines are shortened to the trial end. After day 14, entries stop; existing local exits continue only while running with usable account and market data. Stop retains holdings and overrides automatic recovery. No setting guarantees a maximum loss or liquidation.

Before activation:

1. Review, test and publish the exact source, then back up both stores and deploy paused. Preserve the account binding, funding baseline, ledgers, orders, events, minute archives, schedules and existing protection.
2. With separate operator authorization, POST `/api/qualify-paper` using the normal Origin/JSON/CSRF protection and `confirmation: "ONE ALPACA PAPER ROUND TRIP"`. The initial check requires a paused, reconciled, flat account with no previous local orders. Keep the legacy HTF policy for this check. It claims one durable attempt before submitting anything.
3. The check submits one passive GTC buy, capped at $25 including the planning fee reserve. It observes a 60-second fill window using order-status reads, then cancels any confirmed working remainder without a fallback buy. Before its exit leg it refreshes planning-fee evidence, since the fill window can outlast the fee snapshot. It then attempts one bounded IOC sell of confirmed owned quantity. API latency and settlement can extend the procedure beyond the fill window. Unknown write outcomes require reconciliation, never a resubmission. Full account reconciliation follows confirmed order termination, not a working order's temporary cash reservation. Partial fills, fees and market increments can leave holdings. No fill or retained residual means qualification is incomplete; no extra buy is permitted just to clear dust.
   If the original buy is confirmed canceled and unfilled, a separately authorized operator may use `confirmation: "ONE ADDITIONAL ALPACA PAPER ATTEMPT"` once. The account must first reconcile flat. The original claim and order remain recorded, the additional attempt gets its own run, and no third attempt is permitted by this endpoint. This is not automatic recovery or a retry of an uncertain write.
4. Only a completed flat normal round trip with verified account evidence permits trial Start. Unposted fees do not block qualification or Start when observed debits and conservative reserves satisfy the accounting and risk limits. Qualification uses a separately labeled execution run and does not count as a strategy trade. Preserve its costs and records. Save `htf_policy=multibar-v2` and the authorized recovery setting, leaving the engine paused and unarmed. The user Starts the trial explicitly.

The first real attempt was canceled unfilled after a working-order cash mismatch. The additional attempt filled, then halted before its exit because broker BTC was below the gross fill while no fee activity was posted. [Alpaca documents end-of-day crypto fee posting](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-trading#crypto-spot-trading-fees); an observed balance difference is not a confirmed fee.

A separately authorized operator can POST `/api/qualify-exit` with the interrupted additional attempt's `qualification_id` and `confirmation: "SELL CONFIRMED QUALIFICATION HOLDING"`. This one-use exception requires two matching broker reads, exclusive tracked ownership, known terminal buys and only a bounded base-asset debit within the planning fee reserve. It permits one exact sell payload of at most the broker-confirmed owned quantity and $25, with the existing quote, spread and slippage limits. Stop revokes the permission. It does not arm normal trading, adjust a balance, infer a fee, retry a write or permit another buy. The original failures remain archived. Automatic checks retain unclassified debits and consume any actual posted fees exactly once. Restart requires fresh account verification, not fee publication. Even after settlement, an exceptional manual exit is not a passed normal qualification and does not enable trial Start. That manual outcome remains a failed normal qualification; later fee settlement does not relabel it as a pass.

BTC HTF execution now keeps observed fee-related balance differences in an unclassified settlement account. Portfolio balances and owned quantities are net of those observed debits; posted fee totals remain separate. No fee amount is inferred. Two adjacent matching account, position, complete order and activity reads are required, within at most three passes and 60 seconds. Unknown orders, missing fill evidence, credits and unrelated inventory still block execution. Each new fill has a frozen maximum debit based on its captured planning fee reserve and native increment. Cash rounding is limited to one cent per new confirmed fill and is classified only after actual fee activities arrive; BTC differences are never rounded away.

After qualification and the user's explicit Start, pending fees permit reserve-backed entries only with flat tracked inventory and a new valid entry plan. No pyramiding or taker fallback is permitted. Available entry cash and provisional risk equity subtract possible unposted costs beyond observed debits; actual fee totals remain unchanged until activities post. Outstanding fee allowances are capped at the lower of $12.50 and the configured daily-loss limit. Entry sizing also reserves room for the next round trip's planning costs. The original allocation and execution/risk limits remain unchanged.

Native fill timestamps and fee activity dates isolate accounting periods. Creation or receipt time is not substituted for a fee's date. Each period retains its observed debits, frozen caps, posted amounts and cash-rounding bound; newer periods cannot cover older discrepancies. Undated evidence can match only an unambiguous single period. Late, partial and duplicate records are checked without assuming zero fees or treating midnight as settlement. Smaller actual fees than reserves are normal. Posted activities consume suspense exactly once. Fee records remain account activities, not invented per-order attribution.

Owned exits still require running permission, verified ownership and fresh data. When a buy debit has not yet been observed, its native BTC allowance is retained rather than sold. Stop overrides every local exit. The original 48-hour deadline remains an investigation reminder anchored to the first unsettled order. Reads, new entries, day boundaries, exits and restarts do not reset it. Overdue records produce a warning and notification, not a fees-only trading halt. Ambiguous evidence and excess debits still halt; exhausting reserve headroom blocks new entries. Missing activities never become zero fees or an accounting override.

Routine Alpaca Reconcile is replaced by automatic account checks. Running cycles reconcile as before; paused accounts receive read-only checks at least 60 seconds apart, or 300 seconds after a failed check, with a 60-second pass deadline. Checks cannot submit/cancel orders, acknowledge transfers or resume trading. Working or uncertain orders remain blocked; Stop remains reachable. Matching evidence can clear the accounting block, but explicit Start/Restart, loss checks and qualification still apply. Native observations remain separate and confer no execution authority.

After the retained manual recovery is confirmed closed and the account verifies flat, one separately authorized post-fix check is available through `/api/qualify-paper`: use `confirmation: "ONE POST-FIX PAPER QUALIFICATION"` and the exact `previous_qualification_id`. It claims one new passive entry and bounded owned exit, preserves both original failures and the recovery, and cannot be repeated. A completed normal flat execution with unposted fees remains `execution complete; fee settlement pending` and satisfies the execution prerequisite for Start. Its accounting changes to `complete` only when actual activities reconcile. Later accounting retains the qualification's original cash and fee snapshots rather than attributing subsequent trading to that check. Neither outcome Starts or resumes trading. The user must still Start the trial explicitly.

If that post-fix check is interrupted before creating any order, a newly authorized operator can use `confirmation: "ONE NO-SUBMISSION PAPER QUALIFICATION"` with its exact `previous_qualification_id`. This separate one-use claim requires the original post-fix claim, zero orders attributed to that run, the same three terminal historical orders and fresh complete account verification. It archives the failed check without resetting either claim. Rejected or uncertain order records do not qualify for this path. It is not an automatic retry.

If the no-submission check's buy fills but its exit is interrupted by `FeeUnavailable`, a separate operator authorization permits `/api/qualify-exit` with `confirmation: "SELL CONFIRMED FEE-INTERRUPTED HOLDING"` and that exact `qualification_id`. This one-use sell-only claim retains the earlier recovery claim and requires the same three terminal historical orders plus one confirmed owned buy from the interrupted check, with no exit already recorded. Two matching broker reads must agree with the already-reconciled net ledger. Unwitnessed native BTC allowances remain unsold. The $25 ceiling, source-age/spread/slippage limits and Stop guard still apply. Rejection or uncertainty cannot be retried. Neither manual recovery nor any operational execution purpose creates the strategy trial or turns a failed qualification into a pass.

A verified-flat interrupted qualification is shown as `Paused · qualification`, with Restart disabled and the remaining prerequisite explained. Stop revokes execution permission; it cannot repair qualification. Historical failure details remain in Review and audit records. Real accounting, ownership, loss and audit failures remain halts. Passive entries recheck a fresh same-venue book after account/risk reads without changing the planned limit, candidate or intent expiry; the final transport check still rejects stale or crossing orders.

Review reports operating state, state-change time, service-session durations, last evaluation, scheduled retry status, recorded windows and distinct candidate IDs. Repeated checks are not independent opportunities. Minute health samples and state transitions retain session IDs for later uptime accounting; service downtime is not running time. A read-only recorder retains native windows while paused or waiting without reconciling accounts, calling Jev or creating trading permissions. Missing observations remain missing.

Evaluate the fixed trial against the registered activity gates: ten distinct candidates, five broker-accepted entry attempts and at least 95% running availability outside data/account waits. Order records alone do not prove broker acceptance. Fewer than 30 completed owned lineages leaves economics inconclusive. Report fees versus reserves, marked equity, retained inventory, drawdown, exposure and canceled/partial orders. Compare cash and BTC holding only with their different exposure and costs disclosed. The legacy rule is an observation-only control, not a second execution strategy. Do not tune failed predicates, extend the trial to collect favorable trades or reuse consumed research holdouts.
