# Multi-bar paper trial

[`kairos/trial.json`](kairos/trial.json) registers `alpaca-btc-multibar-001` before prospective execution. Its hash is recorded with each trial run. The strategy uses completed Alpaca US hourly bars, deterministic entries and the existing independent exits. Jev is not called and cannot authorize or veto an order. This is a new experiment, not a repair that establishes profitability.

The trial lasts 14 calendar days from the user's first Start. Stop, outages and service restarts do not extend it. A completed bar can establish a structural candidate even when the current execution quote fails the cost gate. That candidate retains its original target, price bounds and three-hour expiry. Quotes cannot create a candidate, extend its life or revive a consumed attempt. Start/recovery retires pending candidates and establishes a new completed-bar baseline. A rising-trend failure invalidates a pending candidate. Each submission attempt consumes its candidate, including an unfilled or rejected attempt.

Keep the $500 allocation, $25 order ceiling, $100 exposure ceiling and $12.50 daily-loss halt. The 80% exposure target, 100% trim trigger, 300-bps stop, seven-day holding limit and 10-second source-age gate remain. Position deadlines are shortened to the trial end. After day 14, entries stop; existing local exits continue only while running with usable account and market data. Stop retains holdings and overrides automatic recovery. No setting guarantees a maximum loss or liquidation.

Before activation:

1. Review, test and publish the exact source, then back up both stores and deploy paused. Preserve the account binding, funding baseline, ledgers, orders, events, minute archives, schedules and existing protection.
2. With separate operator authorization, POST `/api/qualify-paper` using the normal Origin/JSON/CSRF protection and `confirmation: "ONE ALPACA PAPER ROUND TRIP"`. The account must be paused, reconciled, flat and have no previous local orders. Keep the legacy HTF policy for this check. It claims one durable attempt before submitting anything.
3. The check submits one passive GTC buy, capped at $25 including the planning fee reserve. It observes a 60-second fill window with broker reads, then cancels any confirmed working remainder without a fallback buy. It attempts one bounded IOC sell of confirmed owned quantity. API latency and settlement can extend the procedure beyond the fill window. Unknown write outcomes require reconciliation, never a resubmission. Partial fills, fees and market increments can leave holdings. No fill or retained residual means qualification is incomplete; no extra buy is permitted just to clear dust.
4. Only a completed flat round trip permits trial Start. Qualification uses a separately labeled execution run and does not count as a strategy trade. Preserve its costs and records. Save `htf_policy=multibar-v2` and the authorized recovery setting, leaving the engine paused and unarmed. The user Starts the trial explicitly.

Review reports operating state, state-change time, service-session durations, last evaluation, scheduled retry status, recorded windows and distinct candidate IDs. Repeated checks are not independent opportunities. Minute health samples and state transitions retain session IDs for later uptime accounting; service downtime is not running time. A read-only recorder retains native windows while paused or waiting without reconciling accounts, calling Jev or creating trading permissions. Missing observations remain missing.

Evaluate the fixed trial against the registered activity gates: ten distinct candidates, five broker-accepted entry attempts and at least 95% running availability outside data/account waits. Order records alone do not prove broker acceptance. Fewer than 30 completed owned lineages leaves economics inconclusive. Report fees versus reserves, marked equity, retained inventory, drawdown, exposure and canceled/partial orders. Compare cash and BTC holding only with their different exposure and costs disclosed. The legacy rule is an observation-only control, not a second execution strategy. Do not tune failed predicates, extend the trial to collect favorable trades or reuse consumed research holdouts.
